About Us

Sri Rama Mandira Devasthana Charitable Trust

A public religious and charitable trust based in Bodabanda Village, Andhra Pradesh.

Our Purpose

The Trust works for religious, charitable and community-oriented activities in accordance with its registered objects and applicable law.

Our areas of service include community welfare, education, health awareness, environmental protection, water conservation, agriculture-related initiatives and religious activities.
